FAQ

Short answers to the questions that decide whether this is useful to you.

Do I need an Apify or scraping account?

No. SourcingSignal runs the data collection for you and returns a structured decision. You only need an MCP-compatible AI client and your API key.

What does the verdict mean?

Green means the product clears your target price with acceptable margin and supplier risk. Yellow means viable only under conditions (higher MOQ, lower freight, adjusted price). Red means the numbers do not work at the target price.

Which costs are included in landed cost?

Unit price, inbound freight to your destination, duty (when provided), and platform or payment fees you configure. Duty defaults to 0% unless you pass the real rate for your HS code.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from the receipt link or by emailing us. Your key stays active until the end of the paid period and is deactivated afterwards.

What happens to my data?

We store your subscription email, subscription ID, and a hash of your API key. We do not store the API key itself, and we do not sell or share personal data. Reports are generated per request and not retained.
